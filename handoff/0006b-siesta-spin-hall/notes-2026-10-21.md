# Spin Hall runs of 2026-10-20/21: what was done, what worked, what did not

Context: four spin Hall calculations were made outside the staged plan for a talk, using
the stage-3 Python library over the new engine and the stage-6 (`handoff/0006-agi`)
non-orthogonal program. They are the raw material of handoff 0006b (SIESTA inputs) and
of the spin-Hall word. Nothing here is committed as a golden yet. Everything lives on TCN
(`~/shc_km`, `~/shc_bi/run`, `~/shc_pdse2/run`, `~/pdse2/ex`), scripts in the session
scratchpad `bi/` (to be copied into this folder when 0006b opens). The decision on
2026-10-21 (user): the talk uses the legacy bismuthene result; the porting continues by
stages; this note is the list of difficulties to come back to.

## 2. Defects found in the new code

1. **Stage-3 reconstruction energy axis** (`python/lsquant/reconstruct.py`, five Kubo
   formulas): `energies = x/scale + shift` where `shift` is the dimensionless rescale shift,
   not the centre; wrong for every non-centred window, invisible to the goldens because
   all stage-0 windows are centred. Fix = `(x - shift)/scale`. Assigned: handoff 0004
   review 3 (Agi); the tester adds an off-centre golden (G4c, graphene, BOUNDS -3.5 2.5)
   at the stage-4 closure. `dos_curve` was already right.
2. **ISCHO applier is serial**: the PCG triangular solves in `IschoInverseOverlap` run on
   one core; at 64 800 orbitals the generalised route took more than 2 h for M = 1024 and
   16 vectors. Stage 8 item (parallel preconditioner or a better S^-1 applier).
3. **Spectral width of SIESTA bases**: Bi DZP spectrum 81 eV, PdSe2 187 eV (semicore).
   KPM resolution at M = 1024 is 0.25 and 0.5 eV. Either M of several thousand, or a
   smaller basis, or a spectral filter. To be decided in 0006b.
4. `stage6_nonorth` (dd9e908 binary) has `ExactInverseOverlap` capped at 4096 and no
   `--window gershgorin`; the k-block operators needed explicit windows from the exact
   spectrum. The review-1 version prints applier residuals (not used here).

## 3. Pipeline pitfalls (SIESTA, Wannier, wannier2sparse)

1. **SIESTA stores H with E_F = 0** in TSHS/HSX; `read_fermi_level()` returns the printed
   E_F (-3.06 eV for Bi), which must not be subtracted again. Found because the Bi gap sat
   at [-0.13, +0.17] eV in the stored H while the "E_F-relative" DOS showed a metal.
2. **SIESTA band-lines block hangs** in the conda MPI build after SCF (`BandLines` with
   `Diag.ParallelOverK`); the HSX/TSHS were already written, so the run was killed and
   used.
3. **wannier2sparse label-route spin current is zero** for SOC Wannier functions without
   `_s+_/_s-_` tags; `JXSZ.CSR` came out with nnz = 0. Build J = 1/2 {VX, SZexact} from
   the exact spin instead (done in Python, 24 M nonzeros, Hermitian to 4e-17).
4. **pw2wannier90 must write the formatted `.spn`** (`spn_formatted = .true.`);
   wannier2sparse asserts on the binary one (`read_spn: nb == num_bands`).
5. **Wannier90 writes `<seed>_centres.xyz` with atoms included**; wannier2sparse wants
   `<seed>.xyz` with the 12 centres only (`assert_equal 18 != 12`) and a `<seed>.uc`
   that Wannier90 does not write (take it from `unit_cell_cart`).
6. **Wannierisation quality**: with the tutorial's 10/10 iterations the spreads are
   7.6-8.5 A^2; with 300/500 iterations 5.8-9.8 A^2. The 12-band model has no clean gap in
   the conduction manifold at 1.2 eV in these runs, so the tutorial's high-energy
   non-trivial phase is not reproduced; the user attributes this to the velocity
   (interband matrix elements) rather than to the gap; to be revisited with the covariant
   velocity (`_r.dat` was produced) and with the Berry-connection term of theory.md eq. 4.
7. **Gershgorin windows on Wannier Hamiltonians** are 4x wider than the spectrum
   (spread-out hoppings): use the eigenvalue range.
8. **Killed process**: the first Bi M = 1024 run died after 2 h without output (no error,
   `set -e` ended the script); cause not found (not the stage-6 tester). Relaunched.
9. **A running bash script must not be overwritten** (bash reads it incrementally): the
   PdSe2 pipeline died with a syntax error when `run_shc.sh` was replaced under it.
10. **pkill patterns match the ssh shell that launches them** (`pkill -f run_x.sh` kills
    the caller); use `[x]` bracket tricks or exact process names.

## 4. Open physics questions for 0006b

- Non-orthogonal velocity in practice: V = dH - 1/2 (dS S^-1 H + H S^-1 dS) in the atomic
  gauge of sisl was used; the intra-cell Berry connection term (theory.md eq. 4, the
  bracket) was not. Whether it matters for the Bi plateau (6.09) and for the PdSe2
  high-energy phase is the first thing to test: compare against the covariant Wannier
  velocity on the same material.
- Units: the legacy bismuthene curve is labelled e^2/h but matches the new value divided
  by 2 pi; settle one convention (Pauli sigma_z, e^2/h) and write it into the word.
- k-mesh convergence of the exact non-orthogonal reference for PdSe2 (1.40 at 12x12 vs
  1.70 at 24x24).
- Which bismuthene geometry Santiago used (on SiC, a ~ 5.35 A?) for a like-for-like check.

## 6. Addendum (2026-10-21, 04:00 UTC): the KPM material runs were aborted

The three `stage6_nonorth` generalised-route runs (Bi 30x30 k, M = 1024 and M = 4096;
PdSe2 12x12 k, M = 2048) were stopped after 6 to 7 h without output. Estimate: with
`cond(S) ~ 1e3` to `3.5e3` the ISCHO-preconditioned CG at `tol = 1e-10` needs of the
order of 100 iterations per solve, one solve per recursion step on each side, i.e.
`2 M N_states` solves of `~1e7` flops each running on one core: tens of hours. The
exact non-orthogonal references (section 1) stand; the KPM demonstration on a real
SIESTA basis waits for the stage-8 applier work (parallel triangular solves or a
Chebyshev `S^-1` with a good preconditioner, and a looser, controlled tolerance).

## 7. Decision (user, 2026-10-21 10:30): S^-1 exact first, then a study of its expansion

For the real-material route `S^-1` is applied **exactly** (direct factorisation: dense
`LLT` per k block in the Bloch representation; sparse direct Cholesky, `Eigen::SimplicialLLT`
or CHOLMOD, for real-space `S`), not by an iterative or truncated applier. A KPM word on a
non-orthogonal basis is a nested expansion (every step of the outer Chebyshev recursion
applies `S^-1`), so the expansion of `S^-1` gets its own study before it is allowed in a
production path: a handoff ("0006c, the S^-1 expansion") with these experiments on the
rotated fixtures (cond 5 to 7), a cond ~100 fixture and the real Bi `S` (cond ~3500):
(a) Chebyshev coefficient decay of `1/x` on `[lambda_min, lambda_max]` vs the condition
number, against `n ~ (sqrt(kappa)/2) ln(1/eps)`; (b) the error of the truncated `S^-1`
propagated into the DOS and Kubo moments as a function of the order and of the moment
index (the non-Hermitian perturbation of `S^-1 H`); (c) sensitivity to the interval padding,
asymmetric (an eigenvalue below `a` diverges); (d) Chebyshev on the preconditioned operator
`M^-1 S` (block Jacobi, incomplete Cholesky, sparse approximate inverse) and its cost in
parallel SpMVs; (e) the total cost model `M x order` against the exact factorisation;
(f) which combination reproduces the exact-`S^-1` goldens to 1e-12 and at what price.
Byte-level goldens of the non-orthogonal word are generated with the exact `S^-1`.
